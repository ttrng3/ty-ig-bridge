Ty IG Bridge

A personal tool used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

It moves Instagram link requests from a single folder in the owner's Google Drive to the owner's own Mac, and writes the Mac's answer back to that folder.

Privacy policy

  • The tool signs in to Google Drive only with the owner's own account.
  • It reads and writes plain text files in one Drive folder, the owner's Instagram inbox. It does not open other files.
  • Those files stay in the owner's Drive and on the owner's Mac. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to anyone else.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's Mac and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Requests and answers are deleted after 7 days.
